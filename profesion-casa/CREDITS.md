## Property photos

`img/p/*.webp` are the photos of the agency's own listings as published on profesioncasa.com (served from its listing provider, forocasas.com / InmoPC), checked 19 Nov 2026. Resized and converted to WebP; no other edits. Two listings' extra photos carrying a third-party portal watermark were left out. These belong to Profesión Casa (or its photographers) and are used only inside this private pitch concept; they must not be published elsewhere without the agency's permission.

## Content

- Facts (name, history since 1987 / 2015, services, offices, phones, emails, address) come from profesioncasa.com; Google rating 4.4 from 53 reviews and opening at 09:30 from the Google listing (checked 19 Nov 2026).
- Listings (reference, type, area, price, previous price, sizes, rooms, floor, year, orientation, description) are copied from the listings published on profesioncasa.com on 19 Nov 2026. Short highlight bullets and English summaries were written from those facts. Ref. A1014's detail page was not available, so only its list-card data is shown.
- The €/m² colours on the map are averages of the asking prices of those listings, not market data.
- The mortgage simulation, buying-cost estimate and foreign-buyer guide are general guidance written for this concept and marked as such.
