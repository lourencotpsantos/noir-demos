## Map data

The illustrated map (`js/mapsvg.js`, `js/mapdata.js`) was drawn from **OpenStreetMap** data (coastline, Guadalmedina river, beaches, barrio and district boundaries, metro stations), © OpenStreetMap contributors, **ODbL 1.0**. It is credited on the map and in the footer. Landmark positions (Gibralfaro, Alcazaba, Catedral, port) are approximate. Pins mark the neighbourhood, not the address. The walking times are straight-line OSM distances × 1.3 at 80 m/min, rounded to 5 minutes. The Malagueta beach point was added by hand because it was missing from the query.

## Property photos

`img/p/*.webp` are the photos of the agency's own listings as published on profesioncasa.com (served from its listing provider, forocasas.com / InmoPC), checked 19 Nov 2026. They were resized and converted to WebP with no other edits. Extra photos carrying a third-party portal watermark were left out. These photos belong to Profesión Casa (or its photographers) and are used only in this private pitch concept.

## Content

- Facts come from profesioncasa.com, including the history (since 1987; "Profesión Casa nace en el 2015 en Málaga Capital", so "desde 2015" is confirmed), services, offices, phones and emails. The Google rating of 4.4 from 53 reviews and the 09:30 opening come from the Google listing. All were checked on 19 Nov 2026.
- The listings are copied from the listings published on profesioncasa.com on 19 Nov 2026. The highlight bullets and English summaries were written from them. The detail page for Ref. A1014 was not available.
- The €/m² colours and heights on the map are averages of those listings' asking prices, not market data.
- The neighbourhood descriptions are general and verifiable (beaches, metro, landmarks). The mortgage, buying-cost and foreign-buyer content is general guidance, marked as such.
