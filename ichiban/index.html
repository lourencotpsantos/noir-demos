物語Our story

From a small sushi bar on the Paseo Marítimo to a complete Japanese kitchen.

Ichiban began as a sushi bar by the sea. On 15 September 2015 it moved to Avenida Antonio Belón, and with the move came the full menu: nigiri, sashimi, special maki, traditional starters, tempura, steak… and the teppanyaki table.

  1. Before

    A small sushi bar on Marbella’s Paseo Marítimo.

  2. 15 Sep 2015

    A new home at Av. Antonio Belón 22 and a complete Japanese kitchen.

  3. Today

    Sushi, sashimi, tempura, steak on the hot stone and teppanyaki before your eyes.

鉄板焼きThe teppanyaki experience

Dinner is the show.

In Google reviews, “teppanyaki” comes up 82 times and “show cooking” 18. Here is how it unfolds, step by step.

Illustration of a classic teppanyaki show

  1. 01

    Take your seat at the plate

    The teppanyaki table is a hot steel plate with guests seated around it. Ask for it when you book.

  2. 02

    The chef cooks in front of you

    No hidden kitchen: the chef prepares every dish on the plate, an arm’s length away.

  3. 03

    Fire, steam and sizzle

    Salmon, teriyaki chicken, tenderloin, prawns or scallops, straight off the hot steel and always served with broccoli.

  4. 04

    Or steak on the hot stone

    Premium beef also comes on a hot stone: the Sapporo menu pairs it with deep-fried shrimp, mixed sashimi and chips.

      よくある質問FAQ

      Before you come.

      Do you take groups?

      Yes: book on Restoo or, for larger groups, call 952 90 00 06 to arrange the table. The Osaka (€23.95) and Sapporo (€25.95) set menus are priced per person. [Group packages: to be confirmed]

      Can I celebrate a birthday?

      Of course. Mention it when you book (tick “It’s a celebration” above and repeat it on Restoo or by phone) and ask for the teppanyaki table for the show. [Birthday extras: to be confirmed]

      Where can I park?

      We’re on Av. Antonio Belón in central Marbella, where street parking can be hard in the evening. There are public car parks in the centre; walking or a taxi is easiest. [Nearest car park: to be confirmed]

      I have an allergy. What should I do?

      Tell your waiter before ordering: the restaurant’s menu asks for exactly that. Allergen icons on this site are orientative.

      Are you open for lunch?

      According to Restoo, lunch is 13:00–15:00 Friday to Monday, and dinner 18:30–23:00 (Tuesday to confirm).

      Are there vegetarian dishes?

      Yes: vegetarian gyoza, edamame, vegetable tempura, the deluxe vegetarian roll, vegetable yaki soba and more. Use the “Vegetarian” filter on the menu.
