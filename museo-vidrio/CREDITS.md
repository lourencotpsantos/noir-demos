No modern copyrighted artworks are shown (no Chihuly, Dalí, Daum 1980 or other 20th-century designs). For the 1950s, 1978, 1980s, 1990s and 2013 stops the page draws a generic glass silhouette in code (`js/pieces.js`), captioned "Silueta ilustrativa dibujada por código, no de la colección".

### Drawn by code

- Hero and walk light: a WebGL caustics shader written for this demo (`js/caustics.js`), based on the well-known iterative "water caustic" technique.
- Stained-glass triptych (`js/vidriera.js`): an original leaded-glass design (rose, medallions, jewelled borders, quarries). It is **not** a reproduction of Ernest Haussaire's window "Jesús sanando a los enfermos" (1899); the page says so under it.
- Icons: simple inline SVG strokes drawn for this demo.

Real photos of the collection would come from the museum.
