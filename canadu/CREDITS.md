## Other assets

- `img/noise.webp`, `img/noise-light.webp`: paper texture tiles generated by Noir Digital (SVG fractal noise, rendered to WebP). No third-party source.
- `fonts/`: Fraunces and Figtree from Google Fonts, latin subset, self-hosted. Both are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
- All illustrations (ingredients, vines, clock, the Plaza de la Merced skyline, the stamp) are original inline SVG drawn for this concept.
