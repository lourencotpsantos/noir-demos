# Credits

## Content sources

- **Wine, gin, tapas, cheese lists, prices, allergens, corkage and the daily Andalusian wine tasting** (€29, every day at 13:00, 1 hour, booking only): transcribed from the bar's own published menu, "PURA CEPA - LA CALA DE MIJAS.pdf". This is the Google Drive file that the bar's Google listing links to as its website. It was read on 19 Nov 2026. A few spelling slips were tidied (for example "Shapphire" became "Sapphire").
- **Address, phone, opening hours (every day 11:00–02:00), Google rating 4.6 from 652 reviews and the review topics**: from the Google Maps listing "Pura Cepa Mijas", read on 19 Nov 2026.
- **"Also in Frigiliana and Ronda"**: from the back page of the same menu ("La Cala de Mijas · Frigiliana · Ronda").
- **Written for the demo, not the bar's words**: the general style notes per wine type, the aroma notes in the tasting flight, the copy of the vine-to-glass chapter, the pairing suggestions (labelled as indicative on the page), and the example events in the calendar (labelled "Ejemplo"). Only the daily tasting is a real event.
- **Placeholders**: bottle prices ("[precio]"), the WhatsApp number, and the format and price of private group tastings ("[por confirmar]").
- No founder names are printed. Those names come from a 2012 site and ownership may have changed.
