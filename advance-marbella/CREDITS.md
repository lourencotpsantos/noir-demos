## Photos

All photos come from Unsplash and are used under the Unsplash License, which allows free commercial and non-commercial use without asking permission. None of them are the school's own photos: they are stock images standing in for real photography of Advance Marbella, and none show the school, its classrooms or its residence.

Originals were downloaded from unsplash.com (the photo page's download link, served by images.unsplash.com) and converted to WebP with `cwebp`. The hero comes in 2400, 1200 and 800 px sizes, plus an 800 px crop for phones. The other photos come in 800 px and 1600 px sizes, or 800 px and 1200 px for the 4:5 portrait crops.
