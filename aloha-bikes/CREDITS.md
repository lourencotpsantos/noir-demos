## Drawings and map

- The bike "blueprint" drawings are original SVG line drawings generated in `js/bikes.js` from typical frame geometry for each bike type (road, hybrid, step-through city, hardtail MTB). They are not the published geometry of the Giant models, and the dimension lines carry no figures.
- The "Tu día en bici" landscape, sun and bike icon are original SVG/CSS made for this demo.
- The route map is an original schematic SVG, not to scale and not based on map data. Distances and climbing figures are approximate and marked "[por confirmar]" on the page.
