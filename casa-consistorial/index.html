Our story

The town hall that became a home

One building on the old town square, from 1868 to today. Scroll through its story.

1868

15 February 1868

The town hall opens

Fuengirola opens the doors of its first town hall here, on the square that still bears the name of the Catholic Monarchs.

The years on the square

At the heart of the old town

The building stands at the centre of Fuengirola's old town, on the square where the town's streets, shops and daily life meet.

2013

A careful restoration

Reconstruction begins. The historic façade is fully restored and the house behind it is rebuilt as a modern hotel.

Today

A four-star boutique hotel

The same address on Plaza de los Reyes Católicos, now with rooms and suites, a pool, a rooftop terrace and Café & Restaurant Consistorio downstairs.

Good to know

Before you arrive

Can't find your answer? Reception is open 24 hours a day.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 16:00 and check-out is by 12:00. If you need an early arrival or a late departure, ask us and we'll do our best.

Is there parking at the hotel?

The hotel has no car park of its own, as it sits in the pedestrian-friendly old town. Public parking is available nearby; ask reception for the best option when you book.

Is the pool open all year?

The outdoor pool is seasonal. Season dates: [pool season]. The patio bar is open to hotel guests all year round.

Do you have an accessible room?

Yes. A Traditional Room is specially equipped for guests with reduced mobility. Mention it in your booking request so we can reserve it for you.

How do I get here from Málaga Airport?

The airport is about 20 km away. The Cercanías commuter train runs from the airport to Fuengirola, and taxis are available outside arrivals. We're happy to help you plan the journey.

Why book direct?

You get our best available price, no booking-site commission, and you speak with the team who will welcome you. Requests are answered by reception.
