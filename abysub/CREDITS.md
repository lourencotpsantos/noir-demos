## Facts and placeholders

Facts come from abysub.com, scubadivingmalaga.com and the Google listing (4.9 from 301 reviews, read Nov 2026). PADI depth limits (Open Water 18 m, Advanced 30 m) are PADI's published standards. Prices, opening hours, per-site depth and level, private-boat details and the next trip stay as visible placeholders until the school confirms them. Review topics are summarised, not quoted. "Tu primer día" describes a typical try-dive sequence, not a confirmed Abysub itinerary. The air gauge in the descent is a labelled simulation; the colour-loss depths (red ~5 m, orange ~15 m, yellow ~25 m) are standard approximations. Species depths in "Lo que verás aquí" are general guidance for these Mediterranean species, not records from Abysub sites. PADI is a trademark of PADI Worldwide.
