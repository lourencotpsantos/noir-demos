## Photos (Imagen ilustrativa)

Both photos are from Wikimedia Commons, by the user Zarateman, released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication; no attribution required, credited anyway). They show the street, not the notary office, its building at no. 6, or its staff. Each is captioned on the page as "imagen ilustrativa", and the footer says so.

Processing: cropped to the upper floors (so that no shop signs or brand logos are shown), converted to grayscale and tinted with a warm "golden hour" colour map in ImageMagick (the light is a grade, not the real time of day), exported to AVIF with WebP fallback. The sky and sun glow behind them are CSS gradients.

## Icons and graphics

Line icons, the rotating seal, the deed, the signature, the folder, the passport and its stamps, the pen-nib cursor and the "lista completa" stamp are original inline SVG/CSS made for this concept. Seals and signatures are decorative and are not the notary's official seal or anyone's real signature.

## Content

Facts (name, address, phone, fax, emails, notaries' names, Google rating 4.3 from 82 reviews, opening at 09:30) come from sanchez-membrado.org and the Google listing (checked 19 Nov 2026). Document checklists, the purchase timeline and the foreign-buyer guide are general, orientative information written for this concept and marked as such on the page; they are not the notary's own published guidance.
