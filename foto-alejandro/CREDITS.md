Room photo edits: the framed panorama that hung on the brick wall in the original photo was painted out by cloning brick courses from the same wall (ImageMagick), and the photo was warmed slightly. `room-light.webp` (window light falling across the wall) and the lamp glow are drawn for this concept. Wedding-day frames are square crops (6×6 film look); the grease-pencil circles, sprocket holes and edge marks are original SVG/CSS.

`rt-before-*.webp` is a deliberately flattened copy of the same photo (lifted blacks, lower saturation, cooler cast, made with ImageMagick) to simulate an unprocessed file for the retouch demo. The page says so.
